#### Section 1. Sections 1 to 11 of this act shall be known and may be

cited as the Agricultural Data Privacy Act. 2

#### Sec. 2. The purposes of the Agricultural Data Privacy Act are to: 3

(1) Protect the privacy of agricultural producers in Nebraska; and 4

### (2) Protect the agriculture industry in Nebraska. 5

#### Sec. 3. For purposes of the Agricultural Data Privacy Act: 6

(1) Acquisitions data means data related to the acquisition of 7

capital, land, equipment, inputs, livestock or any other acquisition of 8

agricultural property; 9

(2) Agricultural data means acquisitions data, care data, crop 10

production data, cultivation data, field usage data, financial standings 11

data, GPS data, historical yield data, irrigation data, maintenance data, 12

marketing strategy and decisions data, purchase sell information, usage 13

data, and yield data; 14

(3) Care data means data related to the acquisition or selling of 15

livestock and metrics associated with such a transaction; 16

(4) Controller means an individual or other person that, alone or 17

jointly with others, determines the purpose and means of processing 18

agricultural data; 19

(5) Crop production data means data related to the practice of 20

protecting the crop yields from different agents including pests, weeds, 21

plant diseases, and other organisms that may cause damage to agricultural 22

crops; 23

(6) Cultivation data means data related to the practice of planting, 24

harvesting, cultivating, or other related practices and the data such as 25

dates operation occurred, metrics regarding method of the practice, and 26

other personally identifiable related practices; 27

(7) Field usage data means data related to commodities planted, land 28

care, irrigation, and land management practices; 29

(8) Financial standings data means data related to financial 30

standings of agricultural production operations including assets and

-2-

liabilities, and other related identifiable information;

(9) GPS data means data related to Global Positioning Systems (GPS) 2

in farm machinery, decisionmaking software, and all other related systems 3

utilizing GPS in agricultural practices; 4

(10) Historical yield data means data connected to individual 5

parcels of land, including yield metrics related to the success of crops 6

cultivated in individual parcels; 7

(11) Irrigation data means data related to the method, timing, and 8

usage of irrigation resources and equipment; 9

(12) Maintenance data means data related to maintenance actions of 10

equipment, including services, service intervals, and other related 11

personally identifiable data; 12

(13) Marketing strategy and decisions data means data related to 13

transactions of commodities, livestock, and land, including marketing 14

plans, marketing actions, and related metrics; 15

(14) Process or processing means an operation or set of operations 16

performed, whether by manual or automated means, on agricultural data or 17

on sets of agricultural data, such as the collection, use, storage, 18

disclosure, analysis, deletion, or modification of agricultural data; 19

(15) Processor means a person that processes agricultural data on 20

behalf of a controller; 21

(16) Purchase sell information means data related to the acquisition 22

or selling of livestock and metrics associated with the transaction; 23

(17) Usage data means data concerning the operation of machinery 24

related to common agricultural practices; and 25

(18) Yield data means data connected to the harvest of commodities, 26

including yield and production metrics, as well as date of harvest and 27

other personally identifiable data. 28

#### Sec. 4. (1) A person may provide written consent to any potential 29

controller of such person's agricultural data that authorizes: 30

(a) The potential controller to process such person's agricultural

-3-

data; or

(b) A third party to process such person's agricultural data on 2

behalf of the potential controller. 3

(2) A person that has provided written consent under this section 4

may rescind such consent by providing a written notice of such rescission 5

to the controller of the agricultural data. 6

#### Sec. 5. (1) A controller shall not: 7

(a) Require any person to submit to any processing of such person's 8

agricultural data without the written consent of such person; 9

(b) Provide any difference in any service, good, benefit, or reward 10

provided to any person who does not consent to the collection or 11

possession of agricultural data; or 12

(c) Sell, provide, or use the agricultural data of any person 13

without such person's authorization. 14

(2) A controller shall delete the agricultural data relating to a 15

person that has provided a written notice rescinding the authorization 16

pursuant to section 4 of this act within thirty days after receiving such 17

written notice. 18

#### Sec. 6. A processor shall not process, sell to any person, provide 19

to any person, or use the agricultural data of a person without such 20

person providing written consent that authorizes such processing to the 21

controller of the agricultural data under section 4 of this act. 22

#### Sec. 7. The Agricultural Data Privacy Act does not apply to the 23

processing of agricultural data by a person if such person is the owner 24

of the agricultural data. 25

#### Sec. 8. Any provision of a contract or agreement that waives or 26

limits in any way any requirement for a controller or processor described 27

in sections 5 or 6 of this act is contrary to public policy and is void 28

and unenforceable. 29
