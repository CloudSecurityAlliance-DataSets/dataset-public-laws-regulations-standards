**Key findings:**
- **SecID-Service runs its full test suite before uploading the registry,** so a registry change can break deploys through a service test. The independent final review caught this (C1).
- **CCM 4.1.1 was shipped without announcement.** The artifact page still says "v4.1, Released 01/27/2026". Only the filename and cell A1 show 4.1.1.
- **The Change Log names 2 of 127 reworded cells.** 101 specifications and 26 CAIQ questions differ, mostly serial commas.
- **4.1.1 is the first CCM 4.1 release with mappings:** NIST 800-171 rev2, MITRE ATT&CK v17.1, and the DORA reverse mapping. 4.1.0's mappings sheet said "This dataset is not available yet". CSA's machine-readable bundle is still 4.1.0.
- **IAM-13 MFA wording changed:** "at least privileged user" → "a least-privileged user". The meaning may have changed; worth a question to CSA.
- **DCS-11.1 now asks about one of the two locations its control names.**
- **SecID could not resolve `ccm@4.1#I&S-01`**, because 4.1 renamed IVS to I&S. This is fixed in #207.
- **AICM is current as far as public evidence shows.** The machine-readable bundle is 1.1.1, matching ours. The main v1.1 bundle now needs a login, so it was not opened.
- **openpyxl `read_only=True` truncates these CSA sheets at column D.** Use full load.
