```
specification_name "Cloud Controls Matrix" · specification_version "4.1.1" (from A1) ·
generated_at "2026-10-02" (from the filename token) · source_workbook <filename> ·
mapping_frameworks ["NIST SP 800-171 rev2", "MITRE ATT&CK"] ·
controls[]: every key of a 4.1.0 control, unchanged names and nesting, plus
  scope_applicability_mappings: {"nist_sp_800_171_rev2" | "mitre_attack":

reverse_mappings: {"dora": {source_name: "Digital Operational Resilience Act",

source_data_notes: {sentinel_values, gaps_in_source}
```
