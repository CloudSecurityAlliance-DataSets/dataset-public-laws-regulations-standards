# Lynx — Canadian Large-Value Payment System

## Overview

Lynx is Canada's high-value RTGS, replacing the legacy LVTS in 2021. Operated by Payments Canada under the oversight of the Bank of Canada. Companion Payments Canada systems:

- **ACSS** — Automated Clearing Settlement System (retail clearing)
- **RTR** — Real-Time Rail (instant payments, in development)
- **USBE** — US Bulk Exchange (CAD/USD bilateral)

## License and access

By-laws are public at payments.ca. Operational and security specifications applicable to participants are members-only.
