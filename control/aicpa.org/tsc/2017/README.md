# TSC 2017

The 2017 Trust Services Criteria are the control criteria established by AICPA's Assurance Services Executive Committee (ASEC) for use in SOC 2 and SOC 3 attestation engagements, plus SOC for Cybersecurity examinations.

- **Name:** Trust Services Criteria for Security, Availability, Processing Integrity, Confidentiality, and Privacy
- **Owner:** American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) — Assurance Services Executive Committee (ASEC)
- **SecID:** `secid:control/aicpa.org/tsc@2017`
- **Source:** https://www.aicpa-cima.com/resources/download/2017-trust-services-criteria-with-revised-points-of-focus-2022
- **Licence:** `LicenseRef-AICPA-Copyright`, publicly redistributable: no
