# Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy

[**Explore Canada's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy: AI for All**](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/artificial-intelligence-ecosystem/overview-canadas-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy "Overview of Canada’s National Artificial Intelligence Strategy: AI for All")

Through the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy, the Government of Canada has invested in efforts to drive the adoption of AI across Canada's economy and society.

## About the strategy

The government launched the Pan-Canadian Artificial Intelligence Strategy in 2017 to strengthen Canada's leadership in AI research and expand the country's pool of skilled experts. In the first phase, the Canadian Institute for Advanced Research (CIFAR) led efforts to attract, retain and train top AI talent, and it encouraged collaboration among Canada's three national AI institutes: Amii, Mila and the Vector Institute.

In the second phase, we built on this foundation by supporting Canadian businesses in developing and using AI and by promoting the adoption of AI standards. The strategy was backed by investments from Budget 2021 and the 2024 Fall Economic Statement, and it has three main pillars: Commercialization, which is focused on helping businesses use AI; Standards, which is focused on advancing AI-related standards; and Talent and Research, which is focused on supporting research and growing Canada's AI talent.

Through these efforts, we are translating Canadian AI expertise into real-world benefits and keeping Canada at the leading edge of innovation.

## **Pillar 1: Commercialization**

**National Artificial Intelligence Institutes**

- The national artificial intelligence Institutes—[Amii](https://www.amii.ca/) in Edmonton, [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/) in Montreal, and the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/) in Toronto—are helping to translate research in AI into commercial applications and grow the capacity of businesses to adopt these new technologies.
- The government supported this initiative with $60 million in funding provided in Budget 2021, with each institute eligible to receive up to $20 million over five years, from 2021–2022 to 2025–2026. In the 2024 Fall Economic Statement, the government provided an additional $24 million over three years, starting in 2025–2026.

**Canada's Global Innovation Clusters**

- [Canada's global innovation clusters](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/innovation-superclusters-initiative/en)—[DIGITAL](https://www.digitalsupercluster.ca/), [Protein Industries Canada](https://www.proteinindustriescanada.ca/), [Next Generation Manufacturing Canada](https://www.ngen.ca/), [Scale AI](https://www.scaleai.ca/), and [Canada's Ocean Supercluster](https://oceansupercluster.ca/)—are strengthening Canada's innovation landscape by accelerating the commercialization and adoption of made-in-Canada AI technologies by businesses in key industries.
- The government supported this initiative with $125 million in funding provided in Budget 2021, over five years, from 2021–2022 to 2025–2026. The 2024 Fall Economic Statement committed an additional $150 million over three years, from 2024–2025 to 2026–2027.

## Pillar 2: Standards

- Through the [Standards Council of Canada](https://www.scc.ca/), the government is supporting efforts to advance the development and adoption of AI-related standards.
- The government supported this initiative with $8.6 million in funding provided in Budget 2021, over five years, from 2021–2022 to 2025–2026.

## Pillar 3: Talent and Research

### **CIFAR**

- [CIFAR](https://cifar.ca/ai/) is enhancing programs to attract, retain and develop academic research talent and maintain centres of research and academic training at [Amii](https://www.amii.ca/), [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/), and the [Vector Institute](https://vectorinstitute.ai/). In addition, CIFAR is renewing its advanced research, training, and knowledge mobilization programs.
- The government is supporting these initiatives with $208 million in funding provided in Budget 2021, over 10 years, from 2021–2022 to 2030–2031.

### **Compute**

- The [Digital Research Alliance of Canada](https://alliancecan.ca/en) is providing dedicated computing capacity for AI researchers across Canada to support the objectives of the strategy.
- The government is supporting this initiative with $40 million in funding provided in Budget 2021, over five years, from 2022–2023 to 2026–2027.

## Other Government of Canada initiatives on artificial intelligence

**[Artificial intelligence ecosystem](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/artificial-intelligence-ecosystem "Artificial intelligence ecosystem")**

The government is bringing together international experts, championing smart regulation, building communities of research, encouraging homegrown talent and supporting a diverse ecosystem of businesses to advance the responsible development and use of AI.

**[Canada's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy: AI for All](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/artificial-intelligence-ecosystem/overview-canadas-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy "Overview of Canada’s National Artificial Intelligence Strategy: AI for All")**

This is the Government of Canada's plan to make sure AI serves people, strengthens businesses and communities, and gives Canada more control over its future.

[**Advisory Council on Artificial Intelligence**](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/advisory-councils-artificial-intelligence "Advisory councils on artificial intelligence")

The council advises the government on building Canada's strengths and global leadership in AI.

[**Canadian Artificial Intelligence Safety Institute (CAISI)**](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/canadian-artificial-intelligence-safety-institute "Canadian Artificial Intelligence Safety Institute")

CAISI is part of the government's plan to support the safe and responsible development and deployment of AI.

[**Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy**](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/canadian-sovereign-ai-compute-strategy "Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy")

Guided by the voices of Canadians, the Canadian Sovereign AI Compute Strategy will make strategic investments in public and commercial infrastructure to ensure that Canadian innovators, businesses and researchers have access to the compute capacity they need.
